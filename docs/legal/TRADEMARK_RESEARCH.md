## Risk notes (framing, not legal advice)

- Software names frequently collide in Class 9/42; a clean directory/web
  sweep does not rule out a pending or registered mark elsewhere.
- WordPress.org directory availability of a slug is a naming resource, not
  a trademark right.
- Using "™" without registration is permitted; "®" is not, until
  registered.

**Do not treat this document as clearance. It records preliminary searches
and the exact remaining steps. Professional clearance by a trademark
attorney is recommended before spending on marketing that hard-codes the
name.**
